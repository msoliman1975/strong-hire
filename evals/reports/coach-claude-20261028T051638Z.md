## What failed, and who owns it

1. **Style (CO-5), coach prompts.** In 6 of the 8 sessions the judge failed the `style` rule. The
   direct coach opens with multi-word acknowledgements ("Thanks, that gives the setting.",
   "Good, that is your own work.") where its guide allows one word at most. The gentle coach
   praises the story ("a meaningful outcome", "a helpful lesson"), which its guide forbids.
2. **IV-3, coach engine or prompt.** When the candidate gives a result with no number, the coach
   moves on to the next part (trade-offs, then lessons) instead of asking for the number again.
   Four sessions failed for this.
3. **Versions (CO-8), `strong_api.takes.versions`.** The writer quotes the coach's reworded story
   parts, not what the candidate said. For example, the candidate said "checkout simply could not
   break" and the version quotes "checkout could not break". The product's check accepts this,
   because it treats the story parts as the candidate's words (`sources(story, takes)`). The
   eval checks against the candidate's turns only, so it fails 7 of 8. Decide which one is right:
   either the writer quotes the takes word for word, or CO-8 accepts the coach's parts and the
   eval threshold changes.
4. **Overclaim (checks_claims).** In the gentle session the coach found that four engineers did
   the work, but the context part still says "I basically rebuilt the whole thing".
5. **Style judge, eval side (S37).** Of the 4 wrong picks, 2 give a reason that names the other
   session as the direct one. That points to a judge problem with the A/B field, not the coach.
   So the 50% understates how far apart the two styles are. Fix
   `prompts/evals/coach_judge_style.v1.txt` or the verdict field before the next run.
