Associate Customer Success Manager
Brightdesk
Austin, TX

Brightdesk sells help desk software to small businesses. Associate customer success managers look after a large group of smaller customers.

What you will do:
- Onboard new customers with group training sessions
- Answer customer questions by email and phone
- Track product usage and reach out when it drops
- Hand renewals to the renewals team

What you need:
- A bachelor's degree or 1 year in a customer-facing job
- Clear written and spoken communication
- Comfort with software tools

Nice to have:
- Zendesk or a similar help desk tool
- Experience at a SaaS company
