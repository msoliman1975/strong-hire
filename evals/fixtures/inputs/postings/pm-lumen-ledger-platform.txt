Product Manager - Developer Platform
Lumen Ledger
New York, NY

About Lumen Ledger
Lumen Ledger provides accounting APIs for fintech startups. More than 600 companies build on our platform.

The role
We are hiring a Product Manager for our Developer Platform group. You will own our public REST API, SDKs and developer docs.

Responsibilities:
• Talk with developers every week and turn their feedback into a prioritized backlog
• Write product requirement documents and API specs with engineers
• Define success metrics for developer onboarding and API adoption
• Plan launches with marketing and developer relations

Qualifications:
• 2-4 years of product management experience
• Experience with APIs or developer tools
• Ability to read code or API documentation
• Strong prioritization and communication skills

Preferred:
• Prior software engineering experience
• Familiarity with accounting or fintech
