Technical Program Manager - Hardware/Software Integration
Harbor Robotics
Boston, MA (on-site)

Harbor Robotics builds autonomous forklifts for warehouses. We are a team of 150 people.

You will coordinate the release of new robot software to customer sites, working with firmware, autonomy, cloud and field operations teams.

Responsibilities
- Run the release train for robot software every two weeks
- Track dependencies across hardware and software teams
- Lead incident reviews after field issues
- Report schedule and risk to the VP of Engineering

Requirements
- 3+ years as a technical program manager or engineering lead
- Experience with hardware or embedded software projects
- Comfort with Jira and release management
- Strong communication with technical and non-technical people

Nice to have
- Robotics experience
- Background in mechanical or electrical engineering
