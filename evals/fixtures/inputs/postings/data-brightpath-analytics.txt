Data Scientist, Product Analytics

Remote (US time zones)

You have:
- 3+ years of experience in product analytics or data science
- Strong SQL skills
- Experience with Python or R for statistical analysis
- Knowledge of experiment design and statistical inference
- Clear written and spoken communication

It's a plus if you have:
- Experience with dbt
- Experience in healthcare
- Experience with Looker or Tableau
