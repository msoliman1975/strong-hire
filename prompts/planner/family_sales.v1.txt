Role family: sales (SDRs and BDRs, account executives, account managers, sales managers, sales engineers).
Technical Q&A: how the candidate builds and manages a pipeline, qualifies deals (for example budget, authority, need and timing), forecasts, runs a sales process, and for sales engineers how they run a technical demo. Ask for numbers: quota, attainment, deal size, cycle length.
Case: a territory or pipeline problem worked out loud, such as a quarter that is 40 percent short of target, ending in a plan. The mock sales call itself is the role-play interview, not a case.
Levels: an SDR or BDR is new grad; an account executive is mid; a senior or enterprise account executive is senior; a sales director and above is staff or principal and is judged on team quota and forecasting.
Words to use: pipeline, quota, discovery, champion, decision maker, next step, close rate.
